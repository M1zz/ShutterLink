Support

Start here if the iPhones won’t connect or the shutter doesn’t fire.

What you need

RoleAppRequirements
Camera iPhoneShutterLinkiOS 17 or later · installed from the App Store
Remote iPhoneShutterLink Remote (App Clip)iOS 17 or later · nothing to install

Both iPhones need Bluetooth turned on and must stay close to each other (usually within 10 m). Wi‑Fi and cellular aren’t needed, but the remote iPhone does need an internet connection the first time it opens the App Clip, to download it.

Connecting for the first time

  1. On the camera iPhone, open ShutterLink and allow access to the camera, microphone, adding to Photos and Bluetooth.
  2. Tap the QR button in the top right to show a QR code and a 4-digit pairing code.
  3. Point the remote iPhone’s Camera app at the QR code and tap the card that appears to open ShutterLink Remote.
  4. Allow Bluetooth access and the iPhones connect automatically. The QR screen on the camera iPhone closes by itself.

Frequently asked questions

The App Clip doesn’t open when I scan the QR code
  • Make sure the remote iPhone is connected to the internet. The App Clip has to be downloaded the first time.
  • You can also connect with the pairing code instead of the QR code. Once ShutterLink Remote is open, enter the camera iPhone’s 4-digit code and tap Connect.
  • Check that App Clips aren’t blocked in Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions.
It’s stuck on “Looking for camera…”
  • ShutterLink has to be open on screen on the camera iPhone. If you switch to another app or lock the screen, the remote can’t find it.
  • On both iPhones, check that Bluetooth is on in Settings › Bluetooth, not just in Control Center.
  • The pairing code changes every time ShutterLink launches. If you reopened the app, connect with the new QR code or code.
  • Bring the two iPhones closer together and tap Try again.
I see “Bluetooth permission denied”

Bluetooth access wasn’t allowed the first time the app ran. Turn on ShutterLink in Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth. The remote is an App Clip, so it may not appear in that list. In that case, scan the QR code again and tap Allow when asked.

The preview stutters or looks blurry

The preview is deliberately scaled down so it can travel over Bluetooth. It’s meant for framing your shot, and the actual photos and videos are saved at the camera iPhone’s full quality. Walls, people or distance between the two iPhones lower the frame rate. Even if the preview freezes, the shutter and other controls keep working.

Where are my photos and videos?

They’re saved to the Photos app on the camera iPhone. Nothing is saved on the remote iPhone. If nothing is being saved, check that ShutterLink is allowed at least Add Photos Only in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

My videos have no sound

Turn on ShutterLink in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Can I take a shot on the camera iPhone without the remote?

Yes. Besides the on-screen shutter button, you can use the volume buttons, or Camera Control on iPhones that have it.

Can more than one remote connect?

Yes. Several iPhones can connect at the same time with the same QR code or pairing code, and any of them can fire the same camera.

The camera iPhone’s screen turns off while I’m shooting

ShutterLink keeps the screen from turning off on its own while it’s running. Pressing the side button to lock it or switching to another app ends the connection, so keep ShutterLink open on screen while you shoot. If you reopen it, just connect again with the new code.

Contact

If the answers above didn’t help or you found a bug, please open an issue on GitHub Issues. Including both iPhone models, their iOS versions and the step where things stopped helps us look into it quickly.
