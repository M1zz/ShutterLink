Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Applies to ShutterLink (iPhone app) and ShutterLink Remote (App Clip)

Summary

ShutterLink does not collect any personal information or send it anywhere. There is no server run by the developer, no account, and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The photos and videos you take are saved only to the Photos library of the iPhone that took them, and the two iPhones talk to each other directly over Bluetooth.

1. What the two iPhones exchange

The camera iPhone (ShutterLink) and the remote iPhone (ShutterLink Remote) connect directly over Bluetooth Low Energy at short range. Nothing passes through the internet or a third-party server.

DirectionContentPurpose
Remote → camera4-digit pairing code; shutter, recording, timer, zoom and camera-switch commandsConfirming the connection and controlling the shot
Camera → remoteStatus values such as shooting mode, recording time, timer and zoom levelShowing status on the remote
Camera → remoteLow-resolution preview video (what the camera sees)Framing the shot from the remote

The preview is streamed in real time only while connected, and the remote does not store it. A new pairing code is created each time the app launches, and only a remote that knows the code can send commands and receive the preview.

2. Permissions

  • Camera (ShutterLink) — to take photos and videos.
  • Microphone (ShutterLink) — to record sound with your videos.
  • Add to Photos (ShutterLink) — to save the photos and videos you take. This is add-only access, so the app never reads your existing photos.
  • Bluetooth (ShutterLink and ShutterLink Remote) — to connect the two iPhones.

You can turn any of these off at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone. Only the feature that needs it will stop working.

3. What is stored on your device

The only content ShutterLink saves is the photos and videos you take, and they go to the Photos library of the iPhone that took them. Beyond that, the app keeps only a few values on the device, such as how many times it has been opened and when it was first installed, and these never leave the device. ShutterLink Remote stores nothing.

4. App Clip

ShutterLink Remote is an App Clip, so it opens without installation when you scan the QR code. Downloading the App Clip is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The address in the QR code contains only the 4-digit pairing code.

5. This website

The About, Support and Privacy Policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub may process access logs such as your IP address under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This site uses no cookies or analytics scripts.

6. Children’s privacy

The app collects no one’s personal information, so it collects none from children either.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change would mean the app starts collecting information, we will announce it on this page and in the app’s update notes before it takes effect.

8. Contact

For questions about privacy, please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
